Privacy Policy — ClearAir Winter

Effective Date: November 18, 2026 · Contact: siddhant23012002@gmail.com

1. Information We Collect

ClearAir Winter helps families and neighborhoods navigate severe winter smog and urban air pollution. Depending on the features you choose to use, we process:

  • Location & Air Quality Queries: City names or approximate coordinates you search or share via browser geolocation to retrieve live air quality and weather telemetry from Open-Meteo.
  • Household Profile Preferences (Optional Sign-In): Home type, heating and cooking fuels, commute mode, indoor air gear, and sensitive-group indicators stored in Firebase Firestore when you save your profile.
  • Google Account & Google Tasks Data (Optional): When signing in with Google via Firebase Authentication, we receive your basic account profile (display name, email, user ID). If you authorize Google Tasks (https://www.googleapis.com/auth/tasks), we access your task lists strictly to view, create, update, or remove your chosen ClearAir tasks when you trigger those actions.
  • Community Board Contributions: Neighborhood pledges and smog reports you choose to publish publicly under your display alias.

2. How We Use Information

  • Provide real-time local AQI telemetry, ventilation windows, and 7-day exposure trends.
  • Generate tailored low-emission routines and indoor air quality recommendations.
  • Sync private household plans across sessions in Firebase Firestore.
  • Export selected clean-air routines to Google Tasks at your explicit request.

3. Google API Services User Data Policy

ClearAir Winter's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. OAuth access tokens are held strictly in browser memory during your active session and are never persisted to browser storage, sold, or shared for advertising.

4. Data Retention & Deletion

We do not sell your personal information. You can delete saved routines, community pledges, smog reports, and exported Google Tasks directly inside the app, revoke access from your Google Account permissions, or request deletion by emailing siddhant23012002@gmail.com.
